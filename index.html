The problem

You don't need another vocabulary list.

You need to understand what people mean, hear how they actually say it, and feel comfortable enough to answer without translating every word in your head.

01 / REAL SITUATIONS

Start with the moment.

Coffee with a friend. Meeting someone new. Making plans. Asking for help. Nahtive teaches language through the situations where you will actually use it.

The Nahtive method

Understand. Use it. Own it.

One continuous learning experience instead of a pile of disconnected exercises.

01

Understand

Know what the expression means, how it sounds, and when a native speaker would actually use it.

02

Use it

Put the language into context before you're asked to produce it on your own.

03

Own it

Keep speaking until the phrase stops feeling like something you learned and starts feeling like something you say.

04

Remember it

Your path adapts around what you've practiced, missed, and mastered.
