Useful language

These are the phrases you actually need.

Start with a few high-value expressions you can immediately use.

Suggesting
¿Qué te parece si...?
What do you think about...?
¿Qué te parece si vamos el sábado?
What do you think about going Saturday?
Checking
¿Estás libre...?
Are you free...?
¿Estás libre el viernes?
Are you free Friday?
Changing plans
Al final...
In the end / Actually...
Al final no puedo ir.
Actually, I can't go.
Keeping it casual
Ya vemos.
We'll see / We'll figure it out.
No sé todavía. Ya vemos.
I don't know yet. We'll see.
