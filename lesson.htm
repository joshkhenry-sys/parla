Let's start with something useful

Making plans without sounding textbook.

Today you'll learn the language people actually use when making plans, changing them, and figuring out what to do next.

Real-world situation

You're making plans with a friend.

You want to suggest something, see if they're free, and change the plan if something comes up.

Imagine this:
It's Thursday afternoon. Your friend asks what you're doing this weekend. You have a few ideas, but nothing is completely decided yet.
Useful language

These are the phrases you actually need.

Don't memorize a giant list. Start with a few expressions you can immediately use in conversation.

Suggesting
¿Qué te parece si...?
What do you think about...?
¿Qué te parece si vamos el sábado?
What do you think about going Saturday?
Checking
¿Estás libre...?
Are you free...?
¿Estás libre el viernes?
Are you free Friday?
Changing plans
Al final...
In the end / Actually...
Al final no puedo ir.
Actually, I can't go.
Keeping it casual
Ya vemos.
We'll see / We'll figure it out.
No sé todavía. Ya vemos.
I don't know yet. We'll see.
